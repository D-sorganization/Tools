## Notes

- The `conftest.py` was necessary to expose `src/steam_engine_calculator/python/` on `sys.path` (same pattern as `src/urdf_builder_gui/conftest.py`). Without it, all tests importing `steam_engine_calculator.ui.*` were failing with `ModuleNotFoundError`.
- The `mock_qt_app` fixture in `TestSteamEngineCalculatorMainWindow` was updated to also mock `upstream_drift_tools` — the conftest made the import path resolvable, exposing that the fixture was incomplete.
- The `_create_result_card` method previously only checked `name`; type checks for `key` and `color` were added as they are all `str` arguments per the docstring.
