## Approach Rationale

### InverseDynamics
The Newton-Euler algorithm has two distinct phases: forward propagation (velocities/accelerations)
and backward propagation (forces/torques). Extracting these as `_inverse_dynamics_forward_pass`
and `_inverse_dynamics_backward_pass` reflects the algorithm's natural structure and makes
each phase independently testable. The public `InverseDynamics` function becomes a 39-line
thin orchestrator.

### calculate_pressure_drop
The function is long primarily due to its 19-parameter signature and comprehensive docstring.
The body already delegated cleanly. Extracted `_build_pressure_drop_inputs` to handle
geometry/flow resolution and `PressureDropInputs` construction, reducing the public function
body to 12 lines. The signature could not be compressed without breaking the public API.
The 68L result is dominated by signature + docstring (56L), which is unavoidable.

### _build_ui (RotationConverterTab)
Split into three semantic sections: `_build_input_group` (left panel), `_build_output_section`
(right panel widgets), and `_build_plot_section` (matplotlib canvas). The `_build_ui` method
becomes a 6-line layout assembler. Each helper creates its own widgets and sets `self.*`
attributes as needed by the class.
