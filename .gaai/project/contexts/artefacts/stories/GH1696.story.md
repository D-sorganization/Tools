# GH1696: Assessment: Large __init__.py files act as god modules (up to 611 lines)

**Source:** D-sorganization/Tools#1696

## Description

## Assessment Category: L — Law of Demeter (Module Boundaries)

**Severity:** Low-Medium — Fat __init__.py files re-export too much, creating tight coupling.

### Finding

**__init__.py files over 100 lines:**
1. `data_processor/core/__init__.py` — **611 lines**
2. `model_generation/__init__.py` — 349 lines
3. `pressure_drop_calculator/__init__.py` — 304 lines
4. `signal_toolkit/__init__.py` — 239 lines
5. `rotation_converter/__init__.py` — 208 lines
6. `process_calculators/__init__.py` — 187 lines
7. `theme/__init__.py` — 124 lines (x2, in shared and upstream_drift_tools)
8. `programmatic_pid/__init__.py` — 119 lines
9. `humanoid_character_builder/__init__.py` — 115 lines

### Impact

A 611-line `__init__.py` means every import of `data_processor.core` executes 611 lines of code, pulling in all submodules. This increases import time and couples everything together.

### Recommendation

Use lazy imports (`importlib.import_module` in `__getattr__`) for the largest init files. The `pressure_drop_calculator/__init__.py` at 304 lines contains actual calculation logic that should be in a separate module.
