## Implementation Quality Assessment

problem. The solution adds two complementary mechanisms:

1. **Delta enforcement (T201):** Future print() violations in changed src/ files now fail CI

2. **Full-repo audit (nightly job):** Weekly non-blocking job that accumulates violation
   counts for all four problem areas identified in the story — lint, format, type, print.
   All steps are `continue-on-error: true` per the story's "non-blocking initially" recommendation.

The approach follows the established `Code-Metrics.yml` pattern for informational weekly CI
jobs, reducing the risk of introducing unexpected changes to existing CI behavior.
