# Wind-Estimate Uncertainty and Strategy Analysis

## Scope

The `wind-uncertainty/v1` contract separates the wind that affects the ball
from the wind a player believes is present. It produces deterministic paired
samples and evaluates every declared club/aim strategy on the same true-wind
draws. This common-random-number design makes between-strategy differences
less noisy without claiming the synthetic samples are a site forecast.

## Units, Bearings, and Frames

- Wind speed and velocity use metres per second.
- Landing coordinates use metres in the app plan frame: `+forward` points from
  the ball to the target and `+right` points right of the target line.
- Wind bearings use meteorological **from-bearing** degrees, clockwise from
  `+forward`. Zero is a headwind, +90 degrees comes from the player's right,
  and 180 degrees is a tailwind.
- Internally, wind is converted to the flight frame (`x` forward, `y` left,
  `z` up) as a wind-**to** velocity before aerodynamic integration.
- Launch and aim angles use radians. `crosswind_aim_gain_rad_per_mps` converts
  estimated flight-frame crosswind into an opposing launch-direction change.

## Uncertainty Contract

Each request declares:

- a fixed, uniform, or normal true-speed distribution with a required
  nonnegative lower bound;
- a fixed, uniform, or normal true from-bearing distribution;
- a uint32 seed and trial count;
- player-estimate speed and bearing biases, standard deviations, and their
  correlation in `[-1, 1]`; and
- nonempty provenance describing where the inputs came from.

A negative speed bias models systematic underestimation; a positive bias
models overestimation. Samples are generated by a pinned Mulberry32 and
Box-Muller implementation and quantized to nine decimal places. The shared
golden fixture prevents silent Python/TypeScript random-stream drift.

Normal speed samples and player estimates are clamped at zero rather than
resampled. Bearing samples wrap to `[-180, 180)`. This behavior is part of the
v1 contract and must change via a new schema version.

## Strategy Outcomes and Cost

A strategy supplies a wind-free baseline launch (so different launches can
represent different clubs) plus an estimated-crosswind aim gain. Every
strategy sees the same true/estimated pair in a trial. The simulator uses the
true wind; only the aim decision uses the estimated wind.

For a completed flight, the dimensionless cost is

```text
cost = ((landing_forward - target_forward)^2
      + (landing_right - target_right)^2) / miss_scale^2
```

An integration that does not reach the ground by `max_time_s` is retained as
`nonconverged`. A numerical or contract error is retained as `invalid`. Both
cohorts have null landing coordinates and receive the request's explicit
finite failure cost. They are counted in expected cost and regret, never
silently removed from the denominator.

Expected regret is computed trial-by-trial against the lowest cost among the
strategies on that same true-wind draw. Tied best strategies divide one unit
of probability credit. Output retains every landing point and failure cohort,
so clients can render shot scatter and audit summary denominators.

## Limitations

- The initial web twin evaluates the Waterloo/Penner model; Python can select
  any registered literature model.
- The true-speed and true-bearing distributions are sampled independently.
  The configurable correlation applies to player speed/bearing estimate error.
- Wind varies between trials but is steady within one trial. The underlying
  `wind-scenario/v1` integrator separately supports shear, gusts, and seeded
  turbulence.
- The analysis does not model terrain, roll, obstacles, club-delivery
  feasibility, player-specific dispersion, weather-station calibration, or
  forecast uncertainty. Those require additional evidence and contracts.
- No UI is added in this bounded foundation. PyQt6 and React can consume the
  immutable result to add controls, landing scatter, and strategy tables
  without duplicating the sampling or cost logic.
